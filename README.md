## My first Terraria mod
Leafbound is a small vanilla-friendly expansion that adds garden shears, leaf material, and a leaf armor set. Harvest leaves from trees without chopping them down, craft a full armor set with a +5% movement speed bonus, and unlock a unique stealth mechanic that makes certain flying enemies ignore you while you stand still in full leaf armor.
